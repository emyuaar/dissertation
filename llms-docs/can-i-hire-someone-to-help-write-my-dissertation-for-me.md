## FAQs (Frequently Asked Questions)

### 1. Is a dissertation proposal the same as a research proposal?

No, both are similar in structure, but they serve different academic purposes and audiences.

### 2. What should a dissertation proposal include?

A dissertation proposal should include a research question, objectives, literature review, methodology, and expected outcomes.

### 3. What is the main purpose of a research proposal?

The main purpose of a research proposal is to explain a research idea and show why it deserves approval or funding.

### 4. Do PhD applications require a research proposal?

Yes, many universities require a detailed PhD research proposal during the application process.

### 5. Do Dissertation & Research proposals require a literature review?

Yes, both dissertation proposals and research proposals usually include a literature review to identify research gaps.
